Disenyo Malaya

An organization dedicated to aspiring designers and students who wish to explore and express their creativity in the visual arts.

📅 Founded 2025 🎨 42 Members ➤  MMDC Main Bld., Art Room 1

About Us

Disenyo Malaya is a community for MMDC students who see design as a form of expression and medium for one's imagination, not just a skill or a tool. From Fine Arts and Animation, to UI/UX and branding, we give creatives the paltform to experiment, collaborate, and grow.

How to Join

1

Visit the Studio

Sit in on any open session to see how everything works and meet the team.

2

Submit a Request Form

Tell us what kind of design excites you.

3

Attend Orientation

We'll introduce our tools, workflow, and ongoing projects.

4

Join a Project Team

Illustrator, Editor, 2D/3D Animation, UI/UX, Branding, Painter, Photography, Digital Art, and many more! Let's start creating.
