Journalism

The Digital Fort

The official journalism club of MMDC where you can be updated with current news, articles, announcements, and other relevant information in and out of the university.

📆 Founded 2025 🖋️ 35 Members ➤  MMDC Luzon Hub, Journalism Office

About Us

The Digital Fort exists to inform, engage, and empower the MMDC community through responsible and accurate student-led journalism. We cover campus news, shed a light on student voices, and train the next generation of writers, editors, and visual storytellers through one publication at a time.

How to Join

1

Attend a Session

Drop by any open house or club fair booth to learn what we do.

2

Submit an Application

Fill out a short form detailing your interests, be it writing, art, or editing.

3

Join Onboarding

Attend a quick orientation covering our workflow and club style.

4

Start Contributing

Pick your first assignment and get to publishing.
