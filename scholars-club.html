Scholars Club

A place for MMDC scholars to conenct and collaborate with each other, in order to improve their leadership and academic achivements while establishing a strong support network.

📅 Founded 2026 🎨 26 Members ➤  MMDC South Bld., Meeting Room 3

About Us

Scholars Club brings together MMDC's academic scholars to support one another beyond the classroom. Through mentorship, study collaborations, and leadership training, we aim to help members grow academically while building lasting peer relationships.

How to Join

1

Check Eligibility

Open to students under an active scholarship at MMDC.

2

Attend an Info Session

Learn about our programs and how the membership supports your academics.

3

Submit Membership Form

Fill out a short form confirming your scholarship status and interests.

4

Get Paired With a Peer Group

Join a study circle based on your program and year level.

Activities
